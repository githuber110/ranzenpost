# Code of conduct

Ranzenpost is made by and for parents. Be kind, stay on topic and assume good intent.

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their experience, language or background.
- German and English are both welcome.
- Never post personal data: no logins, two-factor codes, calendar links, names of children, teachers or schools, and no
  screenshots that show them. Remove them before you post, and the maintainer removes them when they slip through.
- No harassment, insults or spam.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete posts and block accounts that break these rules. Report a problem privately
through the repository's **Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**, or by opening a discussion that names no one.
